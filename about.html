Our Origin Story

Welcome to Sprint-Tribe, your number one destination to top quality sprinting gear and everything related to sprinting. Sprint-Tribe was founded in 1994 by Thapelo Mokgantshane in the village of Ramotswa after noticing the struggle of locally talented young boys to afford gear to compete and take their game to the next level. Founded in a small storeroom by an ambitious mind, Sprint-Tribe managed to sky rocket in success, achieving many accolades such as winning Best African sports brand in the year 2007 at the African Excellence awards.

Our Mission

Sprint-Tribe has a mission to bridge the gap between talent and opportunity by delivering affordable, high performance sprint gear.

Our Principles & Vision

  • Accessibility and Affordability: We believe every athlete deserves access to high-quality sprint gear, regardless of financial background.
  • Performance Excellence: We are committed to delivering gear that enhances speed, comfort, and overall athletic performance.
  • Community Empowerment: We aim to uplift and support local athletic communities by providing the tools needed to reach the next level.
