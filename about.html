ABOUT US

Our Origin Story

Welcome to Sprint-Tribe, your number one destination to top quality sprinting gear and everything related to sprinting. Sprint-Tribe was founded in 1994 by Thapelo Mokgantshane in Ramotswa after recognising the struggle of talented young athletes trying to afford professional sprint gear.

Mission

Sprint-Tribe exists to bridge the gap between talent and opportunity by delivering affordable, high-performance sprint gear built for athletes chasing greatness.

Principles & Vision

  • Accessibility: Every athlete deserves elite-quality gear.
  • Performance: Speed, comfort, and excellence fuel every product.
  • Community: We uplift local athletic communities through sport.
